Discharge Decoded

Turn hospital discharge papers into a plain-language care plan: your medicines on a schedule, warning signs to watch for, follow-up visits, and eating/activity guidance. Free, open-source, and self-hostable.

1. Add your discharge papers

Photos of paper pages or a PDF. Text is extracted on this server and personal details are redacted before anything else happens.

…or paste the text

2. Your care plan (plain language)

3. Check your understanding (teach-back)

Explaining your plan back in your own words is the best way to make sure it sticks. Answer each prompt — you'll get coaching on anything missed.

Please read

Discharge Decoded is an educational tool, not a medical device. It can misread documents. Always review your care plan with your doctor, nurse, or pharmacist — especially medicine doses and warning signs. If you feel much worse, get care right away; don't wait for a care plan.
